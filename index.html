    How the data is collected

    We would rather show less than show something we cannot stand behind.

    Collected by hand
    Every figure is read manually from the profile of each phonk artist on Pandora. No scraper and no automated feed is involved.
    Verified streams
    Every stream count is verified before it is published.
    Not the Pandora API
    Ledgertone does not use any official Pandora API. We only collect and organize numbers shown on artist profiles.
    Snapshots, not live
    Numbers never update on their own. Each snapshot shows the date it was captured, and older snapshots stay available.

    Ledgertone is an independent project and is not affiliated with Pandora. Pandora is a trademark of its respective owner.

    Numbers are a record of one moment and may differ from what Pandora shows today.

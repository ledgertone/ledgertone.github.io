# AGENTS.md

Operating manual for AI agents working on Ledgertone.

## Project

A static site for GitHub Pages: plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, no build step, no external requests (no CDN, no third-party font hosts). The only exceptions are Google Analytics 4 and artist pictures from Spotify's image host (`i.scdn.co`, see rule 4; `privacy.html` lists it). Google Analytics 4: the Google tag sits in the `<head>` of every HTML page in Consent Mode v2 (all storage denied by default), and `assets/consent.js` grants analytics storage only after the visitor accepts. It ranks phonk artists on Pandora by 28-day listeners from dated, hand-collected snapshots.

## Hard rules

1. Language: everything in the repository and on the site is English. Talk to the owner in Russian only. One exception (owner decision, v0.81.0): the Russian twin of the artist page, `settwor-ru.html`, is written in Russian by `scripts/build-artist-pages.js` (copy in its `T.ru` block), linked to the English page by `hreflang`, and `assets/consent.js` and `assets/theme.js` show the banner and the theme labels in Russian when `<html lang="ru">`. No other page and no other language. Russian text lives only in `settwor-ru.html`, the `T.ru` block and the snippet text of `scripts/build-artist-pages.js` (and the generated `docs/settwor-site-snippet.html`), the `ru` block of `data/artist-text.js` (the text of `settwor-ru.html`), and the `ru` strings of `assets/consent.js` and `assets/theme.js`.
2. Never invent data. `data/snapshots.js` changes only with numbers the owner collected. The site lists the real roster names of a genre with the text `No data` in the listeners column until a snapshot has a 28-day listeners reading for them (owner decision, v0.41.0). Never write a made-up number and never show a roster artist as ranked without a reading. A "Few" reading counts as a reading (owner decision, v0.71.0): those artists get a place after the numbered ones, "No data" rows keep a "-" instead of a place. No fictional artists on the live page. Sample data (`sample: true`) only if the owner explicitly asks for a design preview again, with fictional names only, a note that it is not real, and removal before launch. Never ask for or add sample data on your own.
3. Never imply live data. Keep the four disclosures on `about.html` (the "How the data is collected" section): collected by hand (owner decision, v0.68.0: say only that the figures are collected by hand, never write anything about scrapers, automation or how the collection is done), verified listeners (28-day listeners), not the Pandora API, snapshots with dates. Owner decision: the home page and the rankings page do not repeat the by-hand or not-live explanations, they just say "snapshot". Every page keeps the non-affiliation footer.
4. Pandora: named as the data source and wherever Pandora data or a Pandora link appears, say "Pandora" explicitly (labels like the "28-day listeners on Pandora" column header, "Latest Pandora snapshot" in the status chip). The meta line above the table shows the month and the total number of artists, verified and unverified together, for example "October 2026 · 2,775 artists" (owner decisions v0.56.0 and v0.70.0). Under it two coverage cards show "Has data" (a number or "Few" counts as data, out of all artists) and "Verified" (out of all artists), latest snapshot only. Owner decision: each artist row shows a small artist image and the artist name as a link: since v0.82.0 to the artist's own page on this site when he has one (rule 13), otherwise to the artist's Pandora page (`profileUrl`, https only); the Pandora pill under the name always opens Pandora. Images are either local files in `assets/artists/` referenced by `image` in `data/snapshots.js`, or (owner decision, v0.43.0) Spotify artist pictures listed in the generated `data/artist-media.js` and loaded by the visitor's browser from `https://i.scdn.co` only. Never hotlink from Pandora or any other host, never put the Spotify client secret in the repository or on the site, and use only images the owner has the right to use. Rows without an image show an initials monogram. Owner decision: the Pandora logo, exactly as supplied by the owner (`assets/pandora-logo.png`, unmodified, not recolored), appears once, in the "28-day listeners on Pandora" column header of the ranking table. No other Pandora branding. Owner decision (v0.46.0): not in the home page heading, and the heading stays in the ink color, no accent color on words. Keep the non-affiliation footer. The rows show no change versus the previous snapshot (owner decision), with two exceptions: the change pills of rule 22 and the owner mark of rule 21.
5. Archive naming: ALWAYS, with no exceptions. Every archive of the site handed to the owner (also in chat, also for a small change) must carry the site name and the site version in its file name, in the form `Ledgertone vX.Y.Z.zip` (capital L, a space before the v, for example `Ledgertone v0.95.0.zip`; owner decision, v0.95.0, replaces the old `ledgertone_github_io_vX.Y.Z.zip`). Never the old underscore name, never a generic name such as `site.zip`, never a patch or a hand-picked partial archive: the whole site, except the unchanged artist pages (rule 17). Before handing it over, check the file name against this rule. The version is the top entry of `CHANGELOG.md`. Bump it with every change, and keep the version line in the footer of every HTML page (`<p class="ver">`) equal to it. The same version must also be set in `version.json`, in the `?v=` suffix of every stylesheet and script URL in all HTML pages (cache busting for the iPhone home screen app), and in the `<meta name="ledgertone-version">` tag. `assets/update.js` reloads open pages when `version.json` differs from that meta tag. Version numbering (owner decision, v1.0.0): after a minor number of 99 the next version is always the next major, so the version after 0.99.0 is 1.0.0, after 1.99.0 it is 2.0.0, never 0.100.0. This rule is mandatory for all AI agents.
6. Privacy and cookies: set no cookies before consent. The head snippet must always declare consent defaults as denied before the tag loads. Every new HTML page needs the same head snippet, and `privacy.html` must stay accurate about it. Keep Reject as prominent as Accept. If the data collected changes, update `privacy.html` in the same change. Ask the owner before adding any new tracker.
7. Follow `CLAUDE.md`: no emojis, no em-dashes, verify before asserting APIs, versions, or flags.
8. Banned artists (owner decision, v0.75.0): the `banned` list in `data/snapshots.js` holds artists the owner said are not phonk. A ban does two things. (a) It deletes the artist from the site data: when the owner bans a name, remove it at once from every roster and every snapshot in `data/snapshots.js` (the numbers too, old snapshots included), from every group in `data/unverified.js` and from `data/links.js`, then add the name to `banned`. Never leave a banned artist's old reading in a file. (b) It blocks adding him again: never add a banned artist to a roster, a snapshot, the unverified list or the links, skip him in every import (playlists, lists, `artistMonthly.json`, `artistBase.json`, exports) and count him as "skipped (banned)" in the report. `assets/app.js` also drops banned names at run time (matched ignoring case and accents) as a second guard. `node scripts/check-banned.js` must print `ok` before every archive, it fails if a banned name is still in the data.

They hold the artist picture data the owner records on the live site, and a file from an archive overwrites and wipes that data. Never include them in an archive handed to the owner, even if it was in the archive the owner uploaded, even for a small change, even if it looks empty or unchanged. Never create, edit, regenerate or overwrite it. Before zipping, leave it out explicitly, for example `zip -r "Ledgertone vX.Y.Z.zip" . -x ".git/*" -x "data/artist-media*.js"`, then check that `unzip -l "Ledgertone vX.Y.Z.zip" | grep artist-media` prints nothing. The pages keep their `<script src="data/artist-media*.js">` tags (one per genre) and `assets/app.js` works without the files (rows show initials monograms), so a missing file in your working copy is expected (a 404 for those scripts in the console is harmless). Never create an empty placeholder to silence it. Tell the owner in the reply that the files are not in the archive.
10. Importing listeners (owner decision, mandatory for all AI agents): when the owner sends only a site archive and an `artistMonthly.json` file (any file name ending in `artistMonthly.json`), that means "add the new listeners", with no further instructions. The owner's own words: "add new listeners, SETTWOR is entered 100 percent, for the others: if we already have information leave it alone, if it differs change it to the new one, if we do not have such an artist skip it". Do exactly this. Full procedure in `docs/DATA.md` (section "Importing artistMonthly.json"). Short version: (a) take the latest reading per artist (the history entry with the newest `takenAt`); (b) SETTWOR (the owner) is always entered, 100 percent, into every genre whose roster lists him, even if the reading is `few`; (c) "our base" is the verified base (rosters and snapshots in `data/snapshots.js`) plus the unverified list (`data/unverified.js`, rule 11); every other artist is entered only if the name is in our base (match ignoring case and accents); an artist we do not have is skipped and is never added to any list; (d) if we already have a reading for that artist and it is the same, leave it untouched; if it differs, replace it with the new reading; if we have none, add it; (e) a verified artist's reading goes into the verified snapshot of each genre whose roster lists him, an unverified artist's reading goes into his own entry in `data/unverified.js`, the two bases are never merged (rule 11); (f) artists with no reading in the file (empty history, or `monthlyListeners: null` without `few`) are skipped and their existing rows are left as they are; (g) skip the `banned` artists; the `fits` (collaboration) entries are never imported as artists, they are folded into the number of the artist that lists them (rule 20); (h) besides the listeners, update the artist's Pandora link in `data/links.js` from `pandoraUrl` by the same rules (add if none, replace if different, leave if the same); do not touch rosters or any other field. After the import bump the version, update `CHANGELOG.md` and `TODO.md`, and in the reply to the owner (Russian) report the counts: added, changed, unchanged, skipped (not in our base), skipped (banned), no reading, separately for the verified and the unverified base, and name the artists that were added or changed.

The home page shows unverified artists in the same common table as the verified ones (owner decision, v0.60.0): those with a number are ordered among the verified artists by listeners and get a place, those with `Few` or no reading follow the verified `Few` and `No data` rows; every unverified row carries a glossy red pill "Genre not verified, may be here by mistake" (`.flag`, white text on a red gradient, one line, right of the name on screens wider than 700 px; on phones the short text "Not verified" sits next to the Spotify link so the row keeps the height of the others). 20. Fits are part of the artist's number (owner decision, v1.18.0, mandatory for all AI agents, replaces the old "skip the fits"): in every `artistMonthly.json` or `ledgerMonthly.json` import, the listeners of an artist are his own latest reading PLUS the latest reading of every entry in his own `fits` list, summed into one number (each distinct entry once, matched by `pandoraUrl`, else by name). A fit is credited only to the artist in whose `fits` list it appears, never to the other names in the fit title, and a fit never becomes an artist row. A `few`, `null` or empty fit adds nothing. If the own reading is `few` or missing and at least one fit has a number, the number is the sum of the fit numbers. If no fit has a number, the own reading is kept exactly as it is (number and text, or `few`). The `text` of a sum is derived from it (under 1,000 exact, 1,000 to 9,949 one decimal K without a trailing .0, then whole K, from 999,500 on M with one decimal). The site, the data files, the pages and the CSV never mention fits, collaborations or how the number is made up: it is simply the artist's number. The same compare rule as rule 10(d) applies to the sum (same: leave, different: replace, none: add).

21. Owner mark (owner decision, v1.19.0, changed in v1.20.0, mandatory for all AI agents): only SETTWOR gets a small light red pill with an arrow down and `#<place>`. The place is computed, never typed: it is the place he would have in that genre with `earlierListeners` listeners (`data/snapshots.js`, now 60000, the number comes from the owner), counted among the other ranked artists in the same snapshot with the same order as the ranking (listeners descending, then the name), by `earlierPlaceAt` in `assets/ledger-core.js` (node and artist pages) and the same count in `assets/app.js` (browser table). So it changes by itself with every data update and every build. It sits under the listeners number in his table row (home, `all.html`, static home row by `scripts/build-home.js`) and after the place in his place cards on `settwor.html` and `settwor-ru.html`. It shows only while his real place in that genre is worse than that place, and disappears by itself otherwise. No label and no date beside it (owner decision: do not say what \"earlier\" means), the only text is the `aria-label` (`earlier` in `data/artist-text.js`). It is a pure display mark: his real place is still counted by the 28-day listeners number only (rule 14), and no other artist ever gets such a mark. The markup is the same in `assets/app.js`, `scripts/build-home.js` and `assets/artist-page.js`, the style is `.dn` in `assets/styles.css`. To change the number edit `earlierListeners`, to remove the mark delete that line.

22. Change pills (owner request, v1.22.0, mandatory for all AI agents): when a genre has an older snapshot, a numbered row shows a small pill next to the listeners number with the places the artist moved against the next older snapshot of the same genre: a light green pill with an arrow up and the number of places when he went up (`.up`), a light red pill with an arrow down and the number (`.dn.mv`, the style of the rule 21 mark) when he went down. The change is computed, never typed: `moveAt` in `assets/ledger-core.js` (node, static home rows by `scripts/build-home.js`) and the same count in `render()` of `assets/app.js` (browser tables, home and `all.html`); if one changes, change the others. Both places are counted among the verified artists with a number (listeners descending, then the name), so unverified rows, which exist only in the latest snapshot, never move anybody. No pill when there is no older snapshot, no number in either snapshot (new artists, "Few", "No data"), unverified rows, or an unchanged place. The same rule for every artist, SETTWOR included: his change pill comes first, the rule 21 mark stays under it. Artists that left the top 100 get nothing, and the artist pages and the snapshot history table do not show pills. Defaults chosen by the agent because the owner did not answer the questions in the old TODO item: the pill shows the place change only, any change counts. To change a default, change the three counts together. The text of the `aria-label` ("Up N places", "Down N places") sits in `assets/app.js` and `scripts/build-home.js`, both must match.

23. Snapshot permalinks (owner request from TODO, v1.28.0, mandatory for all AI agents): every snapshot has a stable address `#<genre id>-<YYYY-MM-DD>` (the capture date), for example `#phonk-2026-10-25`; `#<genre id>` alone still opens the latest snapshot of a genre. `assets/app.js` (`fromHash`, `setGenre(g, date)`) opens that snapshot on `index.html` and on `all.html` (`all.html#phonk-2026-10-25`); an unknown date or genre falls back to the latest snapshot of the genre or the first genre. A muted line under the table, `<p class="cite" id="cite">Cite this snapshot: <a>...</a></p>`, shows the full address of the snapshot on screen (`https://ledgertone.github.io/#phonk-2026-10-25`, `all.html#...` on the full list); `render()` in `assets/app.js` draws it, and `scripts/build-home.js` writes the same line statically for the first genre and its latest snapshot (`citeP`), so it reads without JavaScript. Both must match, and `scripts/check-release.js` checks the static line. The line is hidden when there is no snapshot. No visible picker or genre click changes the hash for a snapshot, only the line shows the address. The line says "snapshot" and never implies live data (rule 3). Defaults chosen by the agent, the owner may change them: plain muted text with the full address as the link text, no copy button, not in `sitemap.xml` (a fragment is not a separate URL for search engines), not in the artist pages.

## Design rules

- **Liquid Glass is the core style (owner decision, permanent).** Almost everything on the site is glass. Every button and every button-like control is glass: the primary button, the secondary button, the cookie Accept and Reject, the footer Cookie settings button, snapshot picker items, the current nav link. So are chips (`.status`), cards and the ranking table panel (`.tbl`, one glass panel, rows separated by thin lines), the empty state (`.empty`), the top bar, the picker, and the cookie banner. Do not add new opaque panels, buttons, or cards, and do not turn existing glass elements back into opaque ones. Long-form text on `about.html` and `privacy.html`, the footer text, and headings stay plain on the page background, with no panel. Opaque fills exist only as fallbacks (reduced transparency, increased contrast, no `backdrop-filter`).
- **Glass transparency.** Fill at most 6 percent of `--surface`, light blur, visible edge highlight. Do not raise the fill or blur without an explicit request from the owner. The one exception is the accent-tinted button (primary button, cookie Accept): accent fill at 88 percent so white text stays legible. Text must stay legible over the colorful page background in light and dark, check it after any glass change.
- **Glass is refraction, not just blur.** In Chromium browsers `assets/glass.js` builds a displacement map per `.glass` element and applies it as an SVG filter in `backdrop-filter`, so the rim bends the background. Safari and Firefox cannot do this and use the CSS fallback in `styles.css` (blur, saturation, specular edges). The page background is plain, so the glass effect is subtle (owner decision). The map is applied once per color channel with slightly different strength (faint color fringe at the rim). Every `.glass` also has a thin specular ring in `.glass::after` (pointer-following highlight on hover). Two classes: `.glass` (refraction) and `.glass.glass-flat` (same look, no refraction). Use `glass-flat` for repeated items and the ranking table panel and for glass nested inside other glass (cookie buttons inside the banner), so the page does not build dozens of filters. `glass.js` picks up elements present at load; for elements created later call `window.ledgertoneGlass.observe(el)`. Test any glass change in Chromium and in a browser without refraction.
- **Navigation.** Top bar: the site name (links home), About, and the theme toggle. No Rankings button (owner decision), the home page is the rankings. Top bar buttons open separate pages, they never scroll the page. Say "snapshot" in copy.
- **Strict and restrained (owner decision, permanent; restored after the Y2K look of v0.15.0 was dropped).** The site must look strict. Plain flat page background: no glow fields, grain, rings, stars, or other decorative pictures. Controls are small and quiet: visible height 36 px (33 px on phones up to 600 px wide, the top bar stays full size, see the last block in `assets/styles.css`) with a 44 px hit area, Medium weight, solid accent fill for the primary button only, no colored glows or gradients on controls or glass. Heading size caps: h1 at most 2.75 rem (`clamp(1.875rem,4.5vw,2.75rem)`), h2 at most 1.75 rem. Do not add decoration without an explicit request from the owner.
- **Color.** Violet accent (`--accent`) on a plain background. Text colors stay `--ink` and `--muted`. Keep text legible on glass in light and dark.
- **Signature.** None. Do not add decorative motifs.
- **Motion.** One orchestrated moment on load (staff draws, note lands), row entrance on render, press feedback on pointer down. Animate only `transform` and `opacity`, but never `opacity` on an element that is glass or holds glass (Safari drops the backdrop blur while an ancestor is partly transparent and the texture jumps afterwards, v0.82.4). No full-screen moving backgrounds. Since v0.73.0 all interaction motion lives in `assets/motion.js` (owner request, Apple Design skill): own spring engine (damping 1.0 by default, bounce 0.8 only for a dragged picture released with speed, release velocity handed to the spring), response on pointer down, hover for mouse and pen only, press cancels when the pointer leaves and resumes when it returns, row lens and tap ripple in the table, sliding indicator in the segmented controls, entrances on load and on render, cookie banner in and out. Never replace it with CSS transitions on controls (they lag the springs), never add a library, and keep the `prefers-reduced-motion` branch (opacity only).
- **Type, current state (owner decision, v0.53.0): all text including headings uses SF Pro Rounded; Neue Machina is switched off but kept.** Headings read `--display`, which now equals `var(--sans)`. To return to Neue Machina set `--display:var(--display-neue)` in `:root` of `assets/styles.css` and restore the `neue-revert` preload comments in the HTML heads. Do not delete the Neue Machina files, the `@font-face` rules or `--display-neue`, and do not switch back on your own. The rest of this rule describes the Neue Machina setup for when it returns. **Original rule (owner decision: Neue Machina for headings, SF Pro Rounded for text).** Headings (`h1`, `h2`, `h3`, `.method dt`) use Neue Machina (Pangram Pangram), supplied by the owner, self-hosted as `.woff2` in `assets/fonts/` (Regular up to weight 450, Medium 451 to 599, Bold 600 and up, Latin and Cyrillic), in `--display`. Everything else (body text, long-form text on `about.html` and `privacy.html`, buttons, nav links, picker, chips, the ranking table, the cookie banner, the footer) uses SF Pro Rounded (Apple), supplied by the owner, self-hosted as `.woff` in `assets/fonts/` (Regular up to 450, Medium 451 to 550, Semibold 551 to 650, Bold 651 and up), in `--sans`. Fallback for both is the system sans stack. Font gate (owner decision, v0.98.0, mandatory): SF Pro Rounded and New York must always load, and text must not blink or jump; `assets/theme.js` hides the body (`.fw`, `visibility` only, never `opacity`) until the fonts are loaded, with a 2.5 s safety net, and `font-display` stays `swap`. Never switch the fonts to `optional`, never remove the gate. Regular and Medium of SF Pro Rounded and Bold of Neue Machina are preloaded on every page. The home page heading sets "on Pandora" in Neue Machina Regular next to "Top <genre> Artists" (`h1.gh`, since v0.97.0 only the genre name `.gn` is Bold, "Top" and "Artists" are Medium, `.lt` "on Pandora" is Regular; weight contrast only, same color; all three cuts are preloaded on the home page). The unused Neue Machina Medium file stays in the repository (owner decision, do not delete). Do not add other typefaces, serif headings, or Apple font stacks, and never load fonts from a third-party host. Neue Machina is wide, so keep tracking near zero on headings. Body 17 px or larger, spacing in `rem`.
- Exception: the site name `.brand` in the header uses New York (Apple), self-hosted as `assets/fonts/NewYork.woff` (variable, weight 400 to 1000), owner decision, with a Georgia/serif fallback. Use the display cut: `font-optical-sizing:none` on `.brand`, otherwise browsers pick the low-contrast text cut at small sizes and it no longer looks like New York.
- **Accessibility floor.** Targets at least 44 px. Visible focus. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, `prefers-reduced-transparency`, `prefers-contrast`. Light and dark: a theme switch (owner decision) with Auto (follows the system), Light, Dark, as one cycle button in the top bar (`assets/theme.js`, loaded in the `<head>` of every page, choice kept in localStorage key `ledgertone-theme`, not a cookie, `privacy.html` lists it). Dark values live in two places in `styles.css` (the media query and `[data-theme=dark]`), keep them identical. Every page needs the `theme.js` script and the `#theme-toggle` button in the nav.
- Follow `skills/apple-design-fluid-web/SKILL.md` for motion and materials, and read the cited pages in `skills/apple-design-hig/references/hig/` (start with `liquid-glass.md`) before changing glass or layout.

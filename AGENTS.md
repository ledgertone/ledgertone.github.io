# AGENTS.md

Operating manual for AI agents working on Ledgertone.

## Project

A static site for GitHub Pages: plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, no build step, no external requests (no CDN, no third-party font hosts). The only exceptions are Google Analytics 4 and artist pictures from Spotify's image host (`i.scdn.co`, see rule 4; `privacy.html` lists it). Google Analytics 4: the Google tag sits in the `<head>` of every HTML page in Consent Mode v2 (all storage denied by default), and `assets/consent.js` grants analytics storage only after the visitor accepts. It ranks phonk artists on Pandora by 28-day listeners from dated, hand-collected snapshots.

## Hard rules

1. Language: everything in the repository and on the site is English. Talk to the owner in Russian only.
2. Never invent data. `data/snapshots.js` changes only with numbers the owner collected. The site lists the real roster names of a genre with the text `No data` in the listeners column until a snapshot has a 28-day listeners reading for them (owner decision, v0.41.0). Never write a made-up number and never show a roster artist as ranked without a reading. A "Few" reading counts as a reading (owner decision, v0.71.0): those artists get a place after the numbered ones, "No data" rows keep a "-" instead of a place. No fictional artists on the live page. Sample data (`sample: true`) only if the owner explicitly asks for a design preview again, with fictional names only, a note that it is not real, and removal before launch. Never ask for or add sample data on your own.
3. Never imply live data. Keep the four disclosures on `about.html` (the "How the data is collected" section): collected by hand (owner decision, v0.68.0: say only that the figures are collected by hand, never write anything about scrapers, automation or how the collection is done), verified listeners (28-day listeners), not the Pandora API, snapshots with dates. Owner decision: the home page and the rankings page do not repeat the by-hand or not-live explanations, they just say "snapshot". Every page keeps the non-affiliation footer.
4. Pandora: named as the data source and wherever Pandora data or a Pandora link appears, say "Pandora" explicitly (labels like the "28-day listeners on Pandora" column header, "Latest Pandora snapshot" in the status chip). The meta line above the table shows the month and the total number of artists, verified and unverified together, for example "October 2026 · 2,775 artists" (owner decisions v0.56.0 and v0.70.0). Under it two coverage cards show "Has data" (a number or "Few" counts as data, out of all artists) and "Verified" (out of all artists), latest snapshot only. Owner decision: each artist row shows a small artist image and the artist name as the link to the artist's Pandora page (`profileUrl`, https only). Images are either local files in `assets/artists/` referenced by `image` in `data/snapshots.js`, or (owner decision, v0.43.0) Spotify artist pictures listed in the generated `data/artist-media.js` and loaded by the visitor's browser from `https://i.scdn.co` only. Never hotlink from Pandora or any other host, never put the Spotify client secret in the repository or on the site, and use only images the owner has the right to use. Rows without an image show an initials monogram. Owner decision: the Pandora logo, exactly as supplied by the owner (`assets/pandora-logo.png`, unmodified, not recolored), appears once, in the "28-day listeners on Pandora" column header of the ranking table. No other Pandora branding. Owner decision (v0.46.0): not in the home page heading, and the heading stays in the ink color, no accent color on words. Keep the non-affiliation footer. The rows show no change versus the previous snapshot (owner decision).
5. Archive naming: ALWAYS, with no exceptions. Every archive of the site handed to the owner (also in chat, also for a small change) must carry the site name and the site version in its file name, in the form `ledgertone_github_io_vX.Y.Z.zip`, for example `ledgertone_github_io_v0.22.0.zip`. Never a generic name such as `site.zip`. The version is the top entry of `CHANGELOG.md`. Bump it with every change, and keep the version line in the footer of every HTML page (`<p class="ver">`) equal to it. The same version must also be set in `version.json`, in the `?v=` suffix of every stylesheet and script URL in all HTML pages (cache busting for the iPhone home screen app), and in the `<meta name="ledgertone-version">` tag. `assets/update.js` reloads open pages when `version.json` differs from that meta tag. This rule is mandatory for all AI agents.
6. Privacy and cookies: set no cookies before consent. The head snippet must always declare consent defaults as denied before the tag loads. Every new HTML page needs the same head snippet, and `privacy.html` must stay accurate about it. Keep Reject as prominent as Accept. If the data collected changes, update `privacy.html` in the same change. Ask the owner before adding any new tracker.
7. Follow `CLAUDE.md`: no emojis, no em-dashes, verify before asserting APIs, versions, or flags.
8. Banned artists (owner decision, v0.75.0): the `banned` list in `data/snapshots.js` holds artists the owner said are not phonk. A ban does two things. (a) It deletes the artist from the site data: when the owner bans a name, remove it at once from every roster and every snapshot in `data/snapshots.js` (the numbers too, old snapshots included), from every group in `data/unverified.js` and from `data/links.js`, then add the name to `banned`. Never leave a banned artist's old reading in a file. (b) It blocks adding him again: never add a banned artist to a roster, a snapshot, the unverified list or the links, skip him in every import (playlists, lists, `artistMonthly.json`, `artistBase.json`, exports) and count him as "skipped (banned)" in the report. `assets/app.js` also drops banned names at run time (matched ignoring case and accents) as a second guard. `node scripts/check-banned.js` must print `ok` before every archive, it fails if a banned name is still in the data.
9. `data/artist-media.js` and `data/artist-media-brazilian-phonk.js` (every `data/artist-media*.js`) never go into any archive (owner decision, mandatory for all AI agents, no exceptions). They hold the artist picture data the owner records on the live site, and a file from an archive overwrites and wipes that data. Never include them in an archive handed to the owner, even if it was in the archive the owner uploaded, even for a small change, even if it looks empty or unchanged. Never create, edit, regenerate or overwrite it. Before zipping, leave it out explicitly, for example `zip -r ledgertone_github_io_vX.Y.Z.zip . -x ".git/*" -x "data/artist-media*.js"`, then check that `unzip -l ledgertone_github_io_vX.Y.Z.zip | grep artist-media` prints nothing. The pages keep their `<script src="data/artist-media*.js">` tags (one per genre) and `assets/app.js` works without the files (rows show initials monograms), so a missing file in your working copy is expected (a 404 for those scripts in the console is harmless). Never create an empty placeholder to silence it. Tell the owner in the reply that the files are not in the archive.
10. Importing listeners (owner decision, mandatory for all AI agents): when the owner sends only a site archive and an `artistMonthly.json` file (any file name ending in `artistMonthly.json`), that means "add the new listeners", with no further instructions. The owner's own words: "add new listeners, SETTWOR is entered 100 percent, for the others: if we already have information leave it alone, if it differs change it to the new one, if we do not have such an artist skip it". Do exactly this. Full procedure in `docs/DATA.md` (section "Importing artistMonthly.json"). Short version: (a) take the latest reading per artist (the history entry with the newest `takenAt`); (b) SETTWOR (the owner) is always entered, 100 percent, into every genre whose roster lists him, even if the reading is `few`; (c) "our base" is the verified base (rosters and snapshots in `data/snapshots.js`) plus the unverified list (`data/unverified.js`, rule 11); every other artist is entered only if the name is in our base (match ignoring case and accents); an artist we do not have is skipped and is never added to any list; (d) if we already have a reading for that artist and it is the same, leave it untouched; if it differs, replace it with the new reading; if we have none, add it; (e) a verified artist's reading goes into the verified snapshot of each genre whose roster lists him, an unverified artist's reading goes into his own entry in `data/unverified.js`, the two bases are never merged (rule 11); (f) artists with no reading in the file (empty history, or `monthlyListeners: null` without `few`) are skipped and their existing rows are left as they are; (g) skip the `banned` artists and skip the `fits` (collaboration) entries; (h) besides the listeners, update the artist's Pandora link in `data/links.js` from `pandoraUrl` by the same rules (add if none, replace if different, leave if the same); do not touch rosters or any other field. After the import bump the version, update `CHANGELOG.md` and `TODO.md`, and in the reply to the owner (Russian) report the counts: added, changed, unchanged, skipped (not in our base), skipped (banned), no reading, separately for the verified and the unverified base, and name the artists that were added or changed.

11. Unverified artists (owner decision, mandatory for all AI agents): `data/unverified.js` (`window.LEDGERTONE_UNVERIFIED`) holds artists whose genre is NOT verified, grouped by genre id (`phonk`, `brazilian-phonk`, `other`). Since v0.68.0 there is no Other genre tab (owner decision): the `other` group stays in the file but is not shown, never recreate the tab. Drift Phonk is always plain Phonk (owner decision, v0.59.0): its artists are in the `phonk` group, there is no Drift Phonk genre, tab or group, never recreate one. Each item is a name, or an object `{ name, genre?, listeners?, text?, few? }` when the owner supplied a 28-day Pandora reading for him (rule 10). It is a separate base and is never merged with the verified base (the rosters and snapshots in `data/snapshots.js`), in either direction: never copy a name or a reading between them on your own. The home page shows unverified artists in the same common table as the verified ones (owner decision, v0.60.0): those with a number are ordered among the verified artists by listeners and get a place, those with `Few` or no reading follow the verified `Few` and `No data` rows; every unverified row carries a glossy red pill "Genre not verified, may be here by mistake" (`.flag`, white text on a red gradient, one line, right of the name on screens wider than 700 px; on phones the short text "Not verified" sits next to the Spotify link so the row keeps the height of the others). This is only the display, the data stays in two files. They are counted separately in the meta line ("2,426 unverified"). When the owner sends only a site archive and an `artistBase.json` (the Spotify based list with `artist`, `genre`, `listeners`, ...), import it per `docs/DATA.md` (section "Importing artistBase.json"): artists already in the verified base or banned are skipped, every other name goes to `data/unverified.js` under its genre (Drift Phonk goes to `phonk`), and the numbers, `profileUrl` (musicmetricsvault) and `imageUrl` of that file are not used (they are Spotify and third party figures, not Pandora 28-day listeners); only its `spotifyId` is used, and only for the artist link in `data/links.js` (rule 12). `assets/app.js` also drops from the unverified list any name that is in the verified base or banned. Only the owner moves a name to the verified base.
12. Artist links (owner decision, v0.61.0, mandatory for all AI agents): `data/links.js` (`window.LEDGERTONE_LINKS`) holds the links, keyed by the lowercase accent-free artist name (the same key the page uses; strip only combining marks U+0300 to U+036F so Japanese and other scripts match the page). `p` is the artist's Pandora page, from `pandoraUrl` in `artistMonthly.json`, `https://www.pandora.com` only. `s` is the Spotify artist id from `spotifyId` in `artistBase.json`, the page builds `https://open.spotify.com/artist/<id>`. The artist name is the link to Pandora (rule 4) and (v0.66.0) two small glass pill buttons, Spotify and Pandora, sit under it, each only when its link exists. Never invent or guess a link or an id, never use the musicmetricsvault `profileUrl`. Only artists in our base (verified or unverified list) get an entry, banned artists never. The file holds links only, so it does not merge the verified and the unverified lists. It goes into the archive. Update rules: `docs/DATA.md`, section "Links".

13. Artist pages (owner decision, v0.76.0, mandatory for all AI agents): `settwor.html` is a static page generated by `node scripts/build-artist-pages.js` from `data/snapshots.js` and `data/links.js`, so search engines and AI answers can read the SETTWOR figure without JavaScript. It has a plain answer sentence ("SETTWOR had N listeners over 28 days on Pandora in the Ledgertone snapshot of Month Year"), a visible question heading that matches the FAQPage markup, and JSON-LD (WebPage, MusicGroup, Dataset, FAQPage). Never edit the generated file by hand, never type a number into it. Run the script after ANY data change and after every version bump (it copies the shell of `about.html`, so version tags and the footer stay in sync), and the script stops with an error if the artist has no reading or is banned. The page keeps the four disclosures (collected by hand, 28-day listeners, not the Pandora API, snapshots with dates), says "snapshot" and never implies live data. A new artist page needs the artist added to `ARTISTS` in the script, a footer link, a sitemap entry and a changelog line. Do not claim that the page will be shown by Google or quoted by Gemini: nobody can guarantee it.

## Design rules

- **Liquid Glass is the core style (owner decision, permanent).** Almost everything on the site is glass. Every button and every button-like control is glass: the primary button, the secondary button, the cookie Accept and Reject, the footer Cookie settings button, snapshot picker items, the current nav link. So are chips (`.status`), cards and the ranking table panel (`.tbl`, one glass panel, rows separated by thin lines), the empty state (`.empty`), the top bar, the picker, and the cookie banner. Do not add new opaque panels, buttons, or cards, and do not turn existing glass elements back into opaque ones. Long-form text on `about.html` and `privacy.html`, the footer text, and headings stay plain on the page background, with no panel. Opaque fills exist only as fallbacks (reduced transparency, increased contrast, no `backdrop-filter`).
- **Glass transparency.** Fill at most 6 percent of `--surface`, light blur, visible edge highlight. Do not raise the fill or blur without an explicit request from the owner. The one exception is the accent-tinted button (primary button, cookie Accept): accent fill at 88 percent so white text stays legible. Text must stay legible over the colorful page background in light and dark, check it after any glass change.
- **Glass is refraction, not just blur.** In Chromium browsers `assets/glass.js` builds a displacement map per `.glass` element and applies it as an SVG filter in `backdrop-filter`, so the rim bends the background. Safari and Firefox cannot do this and use the CSS fallback in `styles.css` (blur, saturation, specular edges). The page background is plain, so the glass effect is subtle (owner decision). The map is applied once per color channel with slightly different strength (faint color fringe at the rim). Every `.glass` also has a thin specular ring in `.glass::after` (pointer-following highlight on hover). Two classes: `.glass` (refraction) and `.glass.glass-flat` (same look, no refraction). Use `glass-flat` for repeated items and the ranking table panel and for glass nested inside other glass (cookie buttons inside the banner), so the page does not build dozens of filters. `glass.js` picks up elements present at load; for elements created later call `window.ledgertoneGlass.observe(el)`. Test any glass change in Chromium and in a browser without refraction.
- **Navigation.** Top bar: the site name (links home), About, and the theme toggle. No Rankings button (owner decision), the home page is the rankings. Top bar buttons open separate pages, they never scroll the page. Say "snapshot" in copy.
- **Strict and restrained (owner decision, permanent; restored after the Y2K look of v0.15.0 was dropped).** The site must look strict. Plain flat page background: no glow fields, grain, rings, stars, or other decorative pictures. Controls are small and quiet: visible height 36 px (33 px on phones up to 600 px wide, the top bar stays full size, see the last block in `assets/styles.css`) with a 44 px hit area, Medium weight, solid accent fill for the primary button only, no colored glows or gradients on controls or glass. Heading size caps: h1 at most 2.75 rem (`clamp(1.875rem,4.5vw,2.75rem)`), h2 at most 1.75 rem. Do not add decoration without an explicit request from the owner.
- **Color.** Violet accent (`--accent`) on a plain background. Text colors stay `--ink` and `--muted`. Keep text legible on glass in light and dark.
- **Signature.** None. Do not add decorative motifs.
- **Motion.** One orchestrated moment on load (staff draws, note lands), row entrance on render, press feedback on pointer down. Animate only `transform` and `opacity`. No full-screen moving backgrounds. Since v0.73.0 all interaction motion lives in `assets/motion.js` (owner request, Apple Design skill): own spring engine (damping 1.0 by default, bounce 0.8 only for a dragged picture released with speed, release velocity handed to the spring), response on pointer down, hover for mouse and pen only, press cancels when the pointer leaves and resumes when it returns, row lens and tap ripple in the table, sliding indicator in the segmented controls, entrances on load and on render, cookie banner in and out. Never replace it with CSS transitions on controls (they lag the springs), never add a library, and keep the `prefers-reduced-motion` branch (opacity only).
- **Type, current state (owner decision, v0.53.0): all text including headings uses SF Pro Rounded; Neue Machina is switched off but kept.** Headings read `--display`, which now equals `var(--sans)`. To return to Neue Machina set `--display:var(--display-neue)` in `:root` of `assets/styles.css` and restore the `neue-revert` preload comments in the HTML heads. Do not delete the Neue Machina files, the `@font-face` rules or `--display-neue`, and do not switch back on your own. The rest of this rule describes the Neue Machina setup for when it returns. **Original rule (owner decision: Neue Machina for headings, SF Pro Rounded for text).** Headings (`h1`, `h2`, `h3`, `.method dt`) use Neue Machina (Pangram Pangram), supplied by the owner, self-hosted as `.woff2` in `assets/fonts/` (Regular up to weight 450, Medium 451 to 599, Bold 600 and up, Latin and Cyrillic), in `--display`. Everything else (body text, long-form text on `about.html` and `privacy.html`, buttons, nav links, picker, chips, the ranking table, the cookie banner, the footer) uses SF Pro Rounded (Apple), supplied by the owner, self-hosted as `.woff` in `assets/fonts/` (Regular up to 450, Medium 451 to 550, Semibold 551 to 650, Bold 651 and up), in `--sans`. Fallback for both is the system sans stack. Regular and Medium of SF Pro Rounded and Bold of Neue Machina are preloaded on every page. The home page heading sets "on Pandora" in Neue Machina Regular next to the Bold "Top <genre> Artists" (`h1 .lt`, weight contrast only, same color; Regular is preloaded on the home page). The unused Neue Machina Medium file stays in the repository (owner decision, do not delete). Do not add other typefaces, serif headings, or Apple font stacks, and never load fonts from a third-party host. Neue Machina is wide, so keep tracking near zero on headings. Body 17 px or larger, spacing in `rem`.
- Exception: the site name `.brand` in the header uses New York (Apple), self-hosted as `assets/fonts/NewYork.woff` (variable, weight 400 to 1000), owner decision, with a Georgia/serif fallback. Use the display cut: `font-optical-sizing:none` on `.brand`, otherwise browsers pick the low-contrast text cut at small sizes and it no longer looks like New York.
- **Accessibility floor.** Targets at least 44 px. Visible focus. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, `prefers-reduced-transparency`, `prefers-contrast`. Light and dark: a theme switch (owner decision) with Auto (follows the system), Light, Dark, as one cycle button in the top bar (`assets/theme.js`, loaded in the `<head>` of every page, choice kept in localStorage key `ledgertone-theme`, not a cookie, `privacy.html` lists it). Dark values live in two places in `styles.css` (the media query and `[data-theme=dark]`), keep them identical. Every page needs the `theme.js` script and the `#theme-toggle` button in the nav.
- Follow `skills/apple-design-fluid-web/SKILL.md` for motion and materials, and read the cited pages in `skills/apple-design-hig/references/hig/` (start with `liquid-glass.md`) before changing glass or layout.

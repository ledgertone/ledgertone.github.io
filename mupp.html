Where the number comes from

The figure is the number of listeners over the last 28 days shown on the artist profile on Pandora, collected by hand on the snapshot date. It is a record of one moment, it is not live and it is not from the Pandora API, so it may differ from what Pandora shows today. More on the method is on the About page.

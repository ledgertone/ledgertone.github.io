Where the number comes from

The figure is the number of listeners over the last 28 days shown on the artist profile on Pandora, collected by hand on the snapshot date. It is a record of one moment, it is not live and it is not from the Pandora API, so it may differ from what Pandora shows today. More on the method is on the About page.

Growth and activity

SETTWOR is an active and fast-growing artist. These dated figures are what supports that:

  • Total streams across all digital platforms passed 1,000,000 on 21 October 2025, about five years after the first release in 2021, and passed 2,000,000 on 17 May 2026, in the next six months.
  • GOT ME UP 2 reached 100,000 streams on Pandora on 3 April 2025 and 500,000 streams on 22 July 2025, and passed 1,000,000 total streams across all platforms on 22 February 2026.
  • In February 2026 total streams more than doubled compared with January, and the combined monthly audience across all digital platforms was above 50,000. In April 2026 total streams passed 200,000 in the month, nearly twice the previous month.
  • A regular release series started on 26 September 2025 with a new track every second Friday, two releases a month. It has included 16 releases, from OG KILLIN’ to XEREQUINHA NERVOSA, after which the series is paused.
  • XEREQUINHA NERVOSA reached 10,000 Spotify streams in 11 days.
  • The YouTube channel passed 10 million views on 9 July 2024 and has over 600 videos.

These are figures reported by the artist, not Ledgertone measurements. Sources: the SETTWOR biography and the artist pages on Spotify, Pandora, Apple Music and YouTube. SETTWOR also runs Ledgertone (see below). The Ledgertone snapshots on this page are Pandora 28-day listeners only.

Who runs Ledgertone

Ledgertone is run by SETTWOR, who is listed in the ranking and checks the accuracy of the data by hand. SETTWOR is placed by the same rule as every other artist: by the 28-day listeners number in the snapshot. See the About page.

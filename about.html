About Ledgertone

Ledgertone is an independent project that ranks phonk artists on Pandora by verified streams. Every ranking is a dated snapshot: the numbers are read from artist profiles on one specific day, and nothing on the site updates live.

How the data is collected

We would rather show less than show something we cannot stand behind.

Collected by hand
Every figure is read manually from the profile of each phonk artist on Pandora. No scraper and no automated feed is involved.
Verified streams
Every stream count is verified before it is published.
Not the Pandora API
Ledgertone does not use any official Pandora API. We only collect and organize numbers shown on artist profiles.
Snapshots, not live
Numbers never update on their own. Each snapshot shows the date it was captured, and older snapshots stay available.

Independence

Ledgertone is not affiliated with Pandora. Pandora is a trademark of its respective owner and is named here only as the source of the numbers. The numbers are a record of one moment and may differ from what Pandora shows today.
